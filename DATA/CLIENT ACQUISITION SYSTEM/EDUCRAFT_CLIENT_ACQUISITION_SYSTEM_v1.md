## WHAT THIS SYSTEM IS

The Client Acquisition System is how EduCraft finds, converts, and retains clients at scale. It answers the question that every other system depends on: **where do the 15,000 projects per year come from?**

Everything else EduCraft has built — WorkBase, the Quality Standard, the Report Production System — is infrastructure. This system fills that infrastructure with paying clients.

## LAYER 1: THE AMBASSADOR ENGINE

### 1.1 The Redefined Role

An ambassador is not "someone with a referral code." An ambassador is an **EduCraft Campus Representative** — a titled, trained, supported position.

**Tier Structure:**

| Level | Title | Requirements | Commission | Additional Benefits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Campus Rep | EduCraft Campus Representative | Complete 30-Day Activation | 10% | Title for CV, branded kit, community access |
| Senior Rep | EduCraft Senior Campus Representative | 6+ conversions, 3+ months active | 12% | Priority support, recommendation letter |
| Campus Lead | EduCraft Campus Lead | 16+ conversions, recruited 2+ new reps | 15% | Quarterly bonus, strategy call invites |

### 1.2 The Parent-Child Ambassador Chain

Ambassadors can recruit sub-ambassadors. This creates a multiplication effect.

**How it works:**

```
Ambassador A (Campus Lead, 15% on own referrals)
    │
    ├── Sub-Ambassador B (Campus Rep, 10% on own referrals)
    │   → A gets 5% from every job B brings
    │
    ├── Sub-Ambassador C (Campus Rep, 10% on own referrals)
    │   → A gets 5% from every job C brings
    │
    └── Sub-Ambassador D (Campus Rep, 10% on own referrals)
        → A gets 5% from every job D brings
```

**Commission math (B's referral):**

```
Client pays: ₦70,000
Worker:      ₦28,000 (40%)
Sub-amb B:   ₦7,000  (10%)
Parent A:    ₦3,500  (5%)
EduCraft:    ₦31,500 (45%)
```

**Rules and caps:**

- Maximum 1 level deep (A recruits B, but B cannot recruit under A)
- Maximum 5 sub-ambassadors per parent
- Parent commission (5%) only activates after parent reaches Silver tier (6+ personal conversions)
- This ensures parents are active sellers, not just recruiters

### 1.4 Ambassador Recruitment Pipeline (With Traqly Scarcity)

The ambassador programme is positioned as exclusive and scarce. You don't just "sign up." You join a waitlist, register on a scheduled date, and earn your place.

**The pipeline:**

```
STEP 1 — RECRUITMENT CONTENT
  EduCraft posts recruitment flier (designed, branded):
  "EduCraft is selecting Campus Representatives for [semester].
   Earn ₦7,000–₦10,500 per referral. Limited positions."
  
  CTA: "Join the waitlist → [Traqly link]"
        ↓
        
STEP 2 — TRAQLY SCARCITY GATE
  The Traqly link controls access:
  - Link has a visitor cap (e.g., 50 people max)
  - Once 50 people have clicked, the link locks
  - Each click generates a one-time password for that device
  - Only that specific device can access the destination
  - Even if the link is shared, other devices cannot enter
  - This creates genuine scarcity: "Only 50 spots available"
  
  The destination is the Ambassador Waitlist WhatsApp group
        ↓
        
STEP 3 — WAITLIST GROUP (Holding Zone)
  Applicants sit in the Waitlist group
  Admin-only posting — no chatter
  Pinned message: "Welcome! Registration opens on [date].
                   Stay tuned."
  Waitlist accumulates 20+ applicants
        ↓
        
STEP 4 — REGISTRATION DAY (Every 2 Weeks)
  Prince posts in the Waitlist group:
  - Welcome video (2 min, pre-recorded, reusable)
  - Explanation of the role, tiers, earnings potential
  - Link to the registration form on EduCraft website
  
  The registration form captures:
  - Full name, phone, email
  - University, department, level
  - Why they want to be an ambassador (1 sentence)
  - Bank details for commission payments
        ↓
        
STEP 5 — AUTOMATIC ONBOARDING
  After form submission:
  - System generates Ambassador ID (EC-A-XXXXX)
  - System generates unique referral code + referral link
  - System generates personalised Ambassador Kit PDF
    (all fliers have THEIR referral link + QR code embedded)
  - Dashboard credentials created
  
  The FIRST thing the new ambassador sees after registration:
  - The Ambassador Kit (download button, prominent)
  - "Welcome to EduCraft" onboarding video
  - 30-Day Activation Challenge instructions
  - CTA button: "Join the Ambassador Hub" 
    → Auto-adds them to the Ambassador Hub WhatsApp group
        ↓
        
STEP 6 — 30-DAY ACTIVATION CHALLENGE BEGINS
  (Detailed below)
        ↓
        
STEP 7 — TRANSITION
  Ambassador is removed from Waitlist group
  Ambassador is now in Ambassador Hub group
  30-Day Challenge tracked via dashboard
```

**Traqly Integration Technical Details:**

```
Traqly API Integration:
  - Create a protected link for the Waitlist group invite URL
  - Set visitor cap (e.g., 50 per recruitment round)
  - Enable device-locked access:
    → On first click: generate one-time password
    → Password grants permanent access from THAT device only
    → Copied link + password on another device = blocked
  - Analytics: track how many people attempted to join,

**"10 Clients = ₦35K Bonus"**

Every quarter (aligned with academic semesters), a time-limited challenge runs:

```
CHALLENGE RULES:
  - Duration: 6 weeks (announced as 4 weeks, extended once)
  - Target: 10 confirmed paying clients
  - Reward: ₦35,000 lump-sum bonus ON TOP of standard 10% commission

  - Those at 5-9 receive standard 10% only (no partial bonus)

  Standard commission: 10% × ₦70,000 × 10 = ₦70,000
  Bonus:                                     ₦35,000

### 2.6 The Formatting Transformation Video Series

Prince's original idea. A screen recording of an EduCraft report being formatted from raw text to final submission, sped up to 60 seconds.

**Production process:**

1. Take a completed EduCraft report (anonymise client details)
2. Copy all text into Notepad (strips all formatting)
3. Start screen recording
4. Paste from Notepad into a blank Word document
5. Format everything: heading styles, page numbers, equations in tables, table formatting, TOC generation, list of figures/tables, margins, spacing
6. Stop recording
7. Speed up to 60–90 seconds
8. Add subtle background music (lo-fi instrumental)
9. Add text overlays: "Raw text → EduCraft quality" (start) + "Your project, done properly" (end)

**Make this a series:** one per project type (FYP, presentation, CV). Each is fresh content.

## LAYER 3: SCHOOL ENTRY

### 3.1 The Student Union Partnership Programme

EduCraft's primary school entry strategy. Instead of cold-recruiting ambassadors, enter schools as a sponsor.

**The model:**

```
EduCraft sponsors a department's student association
  → ₦50,000 per association (₦25,000 deposit + ₦25,000 on agreement)
  → In return: EduCraft rep added to the 500-level WhatsApp group
  → One approved introductory post in the group
  → Association president's implicit endorsement
```

**Why it works:**

- The president is always a finalist → they're in the exact group you need
- Association presidents actively seek sponsors → warm reception
- ₦50,000 is significant for a student association but trivial against the revenue it generates
- You enter as a sponsor/partner, not as a salesperson
- The group has 50–200 finalists who are guaranteed to need project help

**The economics:**

```
Investment: 5 associations × ₦50,000 = ₦250,000
(Paid as 50% deposit = ₦125,000 upfront, balance on agreement)

Breakeven: 8 clients across all 5 departments
  (₦70,000 × 8 = ₦560,000 revenue
   Less worker 40%: ₦336,000
   Less sponsorship: ₦250,000
   = ₦86,000 profit — breakeven exceeded)

Conservative (2% capture from 400 students): 8 clients = ₦560,000
Moderate (5% capture): 20 clients = ₦1,400,000
Optimistic (10% capture): 40 clients = ₦2,800,000

At your 80-90% close rate, you only need 2 people per 
department to MESSAGE you to break even.
```

### 3.2 The Cold Entry Playbook (For Schools Without Partnerships)

When the partnership approach isn't available or doesn't make sense:

```
STEP 1 — Find your first person
  - Search Twitter/X for "[school] final year project help"
  - Search Facebook groups for the school's student community
  - Ask existing clients: "Do you know anyone at [school]?"
  - Ask existing ambassadors: "Does anyone have a friend at [school]?"

STEP 2 — Deliver exceptional work to that first client
  Over-deliver. This is your entry ticket. Include research papers.
  Follow up after supervisor review. Handle corrections immediately.

STEP 3 — Convert the first client into the first ambassador
  "We're launching at [school] and looking for one Campus 
   Representative. Since you've seen our work firsthand, 
   you're the ideal person. Interested?"
  
  A client-turned-ambassador is 10× more effective than a 
  cold-recruited one — they have proof of the product.

STEP 4 — Support the first ambassador aggressively
  Full kit + personal attention for 30 days. Weekly check-ins.
  Celebrate their first referral in the community.

STEP 5 — Let it compound
  2-3 clients → word of mouth → 10-20 clients → second ambassador
```

**Timeline: 3–6 months from first contact to consistent flow.**
